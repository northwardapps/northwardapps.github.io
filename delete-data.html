Data Deletion Request — Granted

Granted does not require an account. Your study progress is stored locally on your device. You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device settings.

What we collect

Granted does not require an account. Study progress stays on your device.

The app also sends data to three processors, described in the privacy policy:

  • PostHog (usage analytics): an anonymous install id, the events listed in the privacy policy, device details, and the IP address (newer builds ask PostHog not to derive a location from it). PostHog privacy policy.
  • Sentry (crash reports and performance traces): crash and trace data, an installation id, and the IP address on the upload. Sentry privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat (purchase state): an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase history (product purchased, transaction date) so Restore Purchases works. No name or email is associated with this data. RevenueCat privacy policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@northwardapps.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request — Granted"
  2. Include the device platform (iOS or Android) and approximate date of purchase
  3. We will process the deletion within 30 days and confirm by email

What gets deleted

  • All RevenueCat purchase records associated with your anonymous ID
  • The PostHog person for that install
  • Sentry events for that install
  • Any support correspondence

What is retained

Anonymized, aggregated transaction records may be retained for tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes as required by law. These records contain no information that can identify you.
